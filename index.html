How to redeem codes

  1. Join Mutant Garden on Roblox.
  2. Open the Codes menu in the game.
  3. Type a code exactly as written (capital letters) and press Redeem.
  4. Each code can be used once per account.

Quick start guide

  1. Plant seeds on your plot and water them.
  2. Weather mutates your plants: Wet, Frozen, Shocked, Gold, Rainbow and Cosmic (up to x50 value). Harvest during an event for big money.
  3. Sell fruit, buy better seeds (the shop restocks every 5 minutes) and visit the traveling merchant at :15 and :45.
  4. Fuse any two fruits in the lab to create mutated seeds.
  5. Fight the Mutant Titan every hour at :30. The whole server fights together.
  6. 🎃 Halloween event until November 3: Blood Moon, haunted plants and limited seeds.

FAQ

Why is the game 16+? New Roblox games are limited to 16+ until they reach enough engaged players. If you are 16+, playing helps unlock it for everyone.

A code doesn't work? It may have expired or already been redeemed on your account. Check the spelling and try again.
